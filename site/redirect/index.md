## Complete remodeling, painting & home care services

### Bathroom Remodeling

A Cape Cod bathroom has to cope with salt air, damp winters and, on a rental property, a whole season of hard use in twelve weeks. We rebuild them so they hold up.

### Kitchen Remodel

Kitchens are the job where sequencing matters most, because every trade waits on the one before it. Running the carpentry, the painting and the final clean with one crew is what keeps a kitchen from sitting half-finished for three weeks while you wash dishes in the bathroom..

### Interior Finish Carpentry

Finish carpentry is the trade the rest of the house is judged by. It is also the one where the difference between good and average is a couple of millimetres at every joint - a mitre that stays closed through a Cape winter, a reveal that runs the same width the length of a wall, a scribe cut to a plaster wall that was never straight to begin with..

### Kitchen Cabinets

Cabinets are only as good as the install. A run of boxes that is a quarter-inch out of level will show on every door gap along the wall, and no amount of hardware adjustment hides it.

### Windows and Doors Installation

Doors and windows are carpentry problems disguised as product purchases. An interior door that binds in August and rattles in February was hung out of plumb, not built wrong.

### Interior Painting

Most of a good paint job happens before any paint is opened. We fill, sand and caulk first, mask the floors and cover the furniture, tape off the trim line, and only then start cutting in.

### Exterior Painting

Coastal exposure is hard on paint. Salt air, summer humidity and New England freeze-thaw cycles will find every unsealed end grain and every bare nail head, and a coat applied over a poorly prepared surface will start lifting inside two seasons..

### Power Washing

Cape houses grow a green film on the north side whether you like it or not. Power washing takes it off, and it is also the step that decides whether an exterior paint job lasts - paint will not bond to a surface with mildew still on it..

### House Cleaning

This is the service that changed how we work. Construction dust does not stay in the room it was made in - it settles on top of door casings, inside light fixtures and in the tracks of every window, and a general contractor who hands the keys back at that point has left you half a job..
